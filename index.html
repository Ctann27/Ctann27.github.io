Consultifa.com: A Documented Comparison of Two Paid Consultations

This page presents a factual record, with evidence, of two separate paid consultations purchased through Consultifa.com, one in November 2025 and one in September 2026. It is published for the purpose of consumer transparency.

Summary

On November 14, 2025, I paid for an introductory reading and a ritual through a contact associated with Consultifa.com, using the name Nicole. On September 21, 2026, I paid again for the same services, this time through a contact using the name Kiara Ward. The video and written guidance I received on both occasions were substantially identical.

Timeline

November 14, 2025

  • Payment of $50 sent via PayPal (Friends and Family) for an introductory reading.
  • Payment of $260 sent via PayPal for a ritual ("Ebo").
  • Recipient name: Nicole.
  • A video of the completed ritual was sent the same day, along with written spiritual guidance.

September 21, 2026

  • Payment of $50 sent via PayPal (Friends and Family) for an introductory reading. PayPal tag: Kiara Ward2000.
  • Payment of $315 sent via PayPal for a ritual ("Ebo").
  • Recipient name: Kiara Ward.
  • A video of the completed ritual was sent, along with written spiritual guidance nearly identical in wording to the message received in November 2025.

Response When Confronted

When I stated that the ritual video sent in 2026 appeared identical to the one sent in 2025, the representative replied:

"Most rituals are done the same way and perform and they look alike. If someone from your end performed the same ritual, the likelihood of the video could look the same... We don't scam here."

When I stated it was the exact same video, the representative replied:

"We don't defraud or perform... Getting the result is what matters the most. The video is simply for your personal record."

Current Status

As of September 21, 2026, I have requested a refund for the September 2026 payments. No refund has been issued. This matter has been reported to the FTC (Consumer Report #207461040) and to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3).
